Depreciation & Allowances — Worked Illustrations

Every worked example backing the Depreciation & Allowances page — drawn from IRD's DIPNs and from Module 9: Principles of Taxation (4th ed.). The 60% initial / 10%-20%-30% annual plant & machinery rates are unchanged in current law, so this topic needed no rate corrections.

A. From IRD DIPNs

Illustration — Industrial building sale: buyer/seller allowance split DIPN 2

A factory is built for $500,000 in February 1992. Initial allowance 20% = $100,000; annual allowance 4% = $20,000 in the first year, continuing for 3 more years, leaving a residue of expenditure of $320,000. The building is then sold for $420,000. The seller faces a balancing charge of $100,000 (sale price exceeds residue by $100,000, capped at allowances previously granted). The buyer's future annual allowance is recalculated as 1/21 (years remaining to the 25th year) × $420,000 = $20,000/year.

Illustration — Plant & machinery pooling across two rate classes DIPN 7

A taxpayer buys a motor car for $150,000 and 3 cookers for $50,000 (both 30% pool), plus office furniture for $20,000 and air-conditioning for $28,000 (both 20% pool). Year 1: 30% pool — cost $200,000, initial allowance 60% = $120,000, annual allowance on the remaining $80,000 at 30% = $24,000. 20% pool — similar mechanics give a smaller annual allowance. Total first-year depreciation allowance across both pools: $176,640.

Illustration — Balancing charge capped at allowances previously granted DIPN 7

On cessation of a business, a 30% pool with a reducing value brought forward of $21,000 (original cost $75,000) is sold for $80,000. Although sale proceeds ($80,000) exceed the reducing value ($21,000) by $59,000, the balancing charge is capped at $54,000 — the total allowances previously granted ($75,000 cost − $21,000 residue) — never more than was originally allowed.

Illustration — Accelerating pre-2018/19 environmental instalments to 100% DIPN 5

Company-HK pays $15M, $20M and $30M in May 2016/2017/2018 respectively for environmental protection installations, under the pre-2018/19 5-instalment (20%/year) regime. Electing under s.16I(3A) to accelerate all remaining instalments to 100% in 2018/19 gives total cumulative deductions of $3,000,000 (2016/17) + $7,000,000 (2017/18) + $55,000,000 combined by 2018/19 (remaining instalments on the first two payments, plus 100% of the third).

Illustration — Specified IP Right deduction spread to match remaining protection DIPN 49

A copyright costing $400,000 has protection expiring in YA2022/23. Rather than the standard 5 equal instalments, the deduction is compressed into the 4 years actually remaining: $100,000/year for YA2019/20–2022/23.

B. From Module 9: Principles of Taxation (4th ed.)

Adapted from a professional taxation qualification manual. Facts are paraphrased, not reproduced verbatim. The plant & machinery pooling rates (60% initial; 10%/20%/30% annual) are unchanged in current law — no correction was needed.

Illustration — Plant & machinery pooling across two years, with disposals and additions Module 9, Ch.3 §3.7.1 — adapted from DIPN 7

A business starts trading on 2 February 2018. In its first year it buys a car ($150,000) and 3 electric cookers ($50,000) — both 30% pool — plus office furniture ($20,000) and 2 air-conditioning units ($28,000) — both 20% pool.

Year 1 (YA 2018/19)
30% pool20% pool
Cost$200,000$48,000
Less: initial allowance (60%)($120,000)($28,800)
Less: annual allowance (30% / 20% of remainder)($24,000)($3,840)
Reducing value carried forward$56,000$15,360

Total Year 1 depreciation allowance across both pools: $120,000 + $28,800 + $24,000 + $3,840 = $176,640.

In Year 2: the car is written off in an accident (insurance + scrap recovery $31,000); a new van is bought for $180,000; a cooker is sold for $3,000 (total 30%-pool disposal proceeds $34,000); an air-conditioning unit is sold for $2,000 and replaced with a new one for $8,000.

Year 2 (YA 2019/20)
30% pool20% pool
Reducing value brought forward$56,000$15,360
Add: new assets$180,000$8,000
Less: initial allowance (60% of new assets)($108,000)($4,800)
Less: disposal proceeds($34,000)($2,000)
Less: annual allowance($28,200)($3,312)
Reducing value carried forward$65,800$13,248

Total Year 2 depreciation allowance: $108,000 + $4,800 + $28,200 + $3,312 = $144,312. Disposal proceeds simply reduce the pool's reducing value — they don't trigger a balancing charge unless they exceed it (see the DIPN 7 illustration above for that scenario). Rates used (60% initial, 30%/20% annual) match current law exactly — no correction needed.
