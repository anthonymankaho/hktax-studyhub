IRD What's New

Everything published on the Inland Revenue Department's What's New page, read against this Study Hub and marked up for what it actually changes.

Source window IRD What's New read on 6 October 2026, covering the items published between 1 June 2026 and 30 September 2026. Since the previous read on 24 September the only substantive item is the Stamp Duty (Amendment) (No. 3) Bill 2026 (29 Sep, §2 below); the other new entries were routine fraudulent-email alerts, which change no tax rule. 1. How to use this page

The Study Hub's tax pages state the law as it stands. This page is the audit trail behind them: what IRD announced, whether it is enacted or still proposed, and which page was edited as a result.

MarkerMeaning
EnactedIn force, or an ordinance already gazetted. Safe to apply, subject to its own commencement date.
Gazetted / billPublished or before LegCo but not yet law. Know it is coming; do not apply it.
ProposedPolicy announcement only, needs an amendment ordinance. Do not apply it to a live filing or transaction.
The trap this page exists to stop A Policy Address measure is not law on the day it is announced. Both 2026 Policy Address measures below are drafted into this Hub as proposed, alongside the enacted figure. Quote the enacted figure in any computation until the amendment ordinance is gazetted.

3. Salaries tax & individuals

2026 Policy Address — child allowance 16 September 2026 · proposed

Child allowance rises from HKD 140,000 to HKD 160,000 for a taxpayer's second and subsequent children born on or after 16 September 2026, claimable until age 25 on the usual conditions. The additional child allowance for the year of birth and the following year rises to HKD 160,000 on the same basis — so HKD 320,000 per qualifying child in each of those two years.

ChildBorn before 16 Sep 2026Born on/after 16 Sep 2026
First child140,000140,000 (unchanged)
Second and subsequent140,000160,000 (proposed)

Intended effect from YA 2026/27, but it requires an amendment ordinance. Two traps: the increase does not reach first-born children, and it is keyed to the child's date of birth, not the year of assessment — an existing second child born in 2024 stays at HKD 140,000 indefinitely. IRD source.

4. Stamp duty

2026 Policy Address — newborn-family concession 16 September 2026 · proposed

A family with a child born in Hong Kong on or after 16 September 2026, at least one parent being a Hong Kong permanent resident, would receive a stamp duty waiver capped at HKD 20,000 on a residential property bought in the window from one year before to two years after the birth. Effective on enactment of the amendment ordinance. It is a capped waiver, not a rate change — on any purchase where AVD exceeds HKD 20,000 the benefit is a flat HKD 20,000. IRD source.

Stamp Duty (Amendment) (No. 3) Bill 2026 — intra-group relief Announced 29 Sep 2026, gazetted 2 Oct 2026 · bill

Relaxes the s.45 associated bodies corporate relief on intra-group transfers in three ways: the association test drops from 90% to 75%; "association" may be established through direct or indirect beneficial interests or voting rights, not only issued share capital; and relief is extended to entities with no share capital to measure, such as limited liability partnerships and companies limited by guarantee. First reading is set for 14 October 2026, and the Bill proposes to apply retrospectively to instruments executed on or after 25 February 2026.

Not law yet. The Stamp Duty page continues to state the enacted 90% test as its primary figure, with the proposed 75% carried as a marked note. The retrospective date matters: if the Bill passes as drafted, instruments already executed since 25 February 2026 that failed the 90% test but meet 75% would become relievable — worth flagging on any live transaction, but not a basis for stamping today. Press release.

Stamp Duty (Amendment) (No. 2) Ordinance 2026 Passed 8 Jul 2026, gazetted 17 Jul 2026 · enacted

Stamp duty on a transfer of dual-counter stock traded at the RMB counter may be calculated and paid in Renminbi, letting an investor settle trade and duty in one currency at one counter. The 0.2% rate is unchanged. Commencement is on a day to be appointed by the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury, pending HKEX system readiness — so it is gazetted but not yet operative. Press release.

8. Fraud alerts

IRD issued fraudulent-email alerts on 6 July (fake legal prosecution notices), 22 July (phishing for email passwords), 4 September (fake tax audit notices) and 15 September 2026 (fake compliance notices and suspicious links), and warned on 25 August 2026 against downloading the eTAX Mobile Application from any unofficial channel.

Rule of thumb for clients IRD does not ask for passwords, does not send payment links by email, and does not distribute eTAX Mobile outside the official app stores. Any "compliance notice" arriving by email with a link should be verified by phoning IRD directly, not by clicking.
